Mission and vision

OASIS advances risk science for sociotechnical systems: power grids, transportation, water and the emergency management that holds them together.

We build quantitative models of how chronic and acute shocks spread through interdependent systems, develop risk-informed decision models, and identify cost-effective adaptation strategies that make critical infrastructure more resilient, sustainable and equitable. We work with emergency managers, utilities, agencies and affected communities, so our models reflect what people actually experience.

Following a disaster from hazard to household

Disasters rarely stay in one system. Our work traces the full chain and uses it to guide better decisions.

  1. 1. Hazard

    Environmental risk

    AI and satellite data to forecast wildfire and the floods and debris flows that follow.

  2. 2. Lifelines

    Cascading failure

    How failures spread across power, water, gas and roads, even with incomplete data.

  3. 3. Households

    Social burden

    Who loses access to what, for how long, and at what cost.

  4. 4. Decisions

    Equitable strategies

    Optimization for hardening, recovery and funding that counts community needs.
