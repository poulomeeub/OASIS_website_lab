Director

Sayanti Mukherjee, Ph.D.

Sayanti Mukherjee, Ph.D.

Associate Professor, Industrial and Systems Engineering, University at Buffalo

Dr. Mukherjee's research is multidisciplinary. She builds quantitative models of systemic impacts on critical infrastructure and communities, conducts risk-informed decision analysis, and proposes adaptation strategies that make the built environment and communities more sustainable and resilient.

She is also Adjunct Associate Professor in Electrical and Computer Engineering and Affiliate Faculty of UB's Institute for Artificial Intelligence and Data Science.
